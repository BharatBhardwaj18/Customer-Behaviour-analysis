-- Customer Behaviour Analysis
A data analysis project exploring customer shopping patterns using Python, Pandas, SQL, and Power BI.

-- What is this project?
This project analyzes the shopping behavior of 3,900 customers to help businesses understand who is buying, what they are buying, and how much they are spending.
 --Tools Used

Python + Pandas — Data cleaning
SQL — Data querying
Power BI — Dashboard and charts


-- Key Numbers

Total Customers → 3,900
Avg Purchase Amount → $59.76
Avg Review Rating → 3.75 / 5
Best Selling Category → Clothing
Subscribed Customers → 27% only


-- What I Found
Clothing dominates — 1,737 units sold, $104K revenue. Accessories is second with 1,240 units.
Most customers are not subscribed — 73% have no subscription. Big opportunity to grow.
Adults buy the most — Age group 35–60 has the highest sales count (1,510 purchases).
Rating is average — 3.75 out of 5. Can be improved with better service or product quality.

-- Business Suggestions

Push subscription offers to non-subscribed customers (73% of total)
Focus marketing on Clothing and Accessories
Improve customer experience to raise ratings above 4.0
Target Young Adults with premium products — they spend well
